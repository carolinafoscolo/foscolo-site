Independent publishing · Belo Horizonte

A publisher shaped
by choices.

Fóscolo & Company Edições is an independent imprint in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, dedicated to history, essays, literature and research.

The catalogue grows where historical inquiry, writing and visual form meet. Publishing involves selecting, organising, revising and finding ways for work to circulate.

Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge opened this path. Todos ou nenhum? — Book I expands the place of poetry and chronicles. The Journal and Repertoire follow questions beyond the books.

A small catalogue, built to last.

The current catalogue begins with two author-led works and grows slowly. A small scale makes it possible to treat research, text, images, production and circulation as parts of the same edition — without making speed a measure of value.

Edited in Belo Horizonte.

The city does not limit Fóscolo’s subjects; it makes visible the point from which the work begins. Archives, the university, Pampulha, libraries and everyday movement through the city form part of what reaches the editorial table.

Independence is not isolation.

The publisher is independent in its editorial decisions, but its books circulate among academic research, public history, cultural institutions, bookshops and readers. It is through those encounters that a work becomes more than a finished file.
