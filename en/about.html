Independent publishing · Belo Horizonte

A publisher shaped
by choices.

Fóscolo & Company Edições is an independent imprint in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, dedicated to history, essays, literature and research.

The catalogue grows where historical inquiry, writing and visual form meet. Publishing involves selecting, organising, revising and finding ways for work to circulate.

Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge opened this path. Todos ou nenhum? — Book I expands the place of poetry and chronicles. The Journal and Repertoire follow questions beyond the books.
