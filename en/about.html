About Fóscolo & Company

Fóscolo & Company is an independent publishing imprint dedicated to works that preserve memory, expand reflection and give lasting form to ideas.


Founded in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, it was born from the meeting of research, writing, culture and editorial care. Its work is guided by a simple conviction: books are more than objects. They are forms of permanence.


Each publication is an opportunity to record trajectories, organize knowledge, preserve experiences and create bridges between authors, readers, institutions and communities of thought.


We live in a time marked by an abundance of information and the fragility of memory. Research often remains restricted to specialized circles. Archives preserve stories that rarely reach wider publics. Institutions produce knowledge that does not always circulate beyond their own walls. At the same time, individual and collective experiences risk disappearing without record, context or recognition.


Fóscolo & Company was created to work within this space.


We publish works related to history, the history of health, intellectual history, public history, essays, society, technology, education, memory, literature, poetry, chronicles and memoirs.


We value projects that combine intellectual autonomy, academic rigor, clarity, careful curation and editorial independence.


Our editorial work is a practice of care: care for authors, sources, language, readers and the stories entrusted to us.


More than publishing books, we seek to build a catalogue: independent, authorial and attentive to the long-term life of ideas.


We believe some ideas deserve to remain.

Mission

To publish and preserve works of history, essays, literature and research, promoting the circulation of ideas, memories and long-term reflections.

Vision

To build an independent and authorial catalogue capable of connecting academic production, essayistic writing and literary creation.


We believe publishing can connect different times, experiences and communities of readers, expanding access to forms of knowledge that help us understand the world with more depth and imagination.

Values

Intellectual Autonomy

We value independent thought, careful interpretation and the freedom to build lasting editorial projects.

Academic Rigor

We publish with attention to research, sources, context and intellectual responsibility.

Clarity

We believe complex ideas can be written with elegance, precision and accessibility.

Permanence

Publishing gives form to ideas, memories and reflections that deserve to endure beyond their immediate moment.

Careful Curation

Each project is developed with attention to language, design, readers and editorial coherence.

Editorial Independence

We build our catalogue with autonomy, responsibility and commitment to long-term cultural value.

What moves us

We publish because some experiences should not disappear.


We publish because certain works deserve time, attention and readers.


We publish because memory, knowledge and culture are essential public goods.


We publish because some ideas deserve to remain.
