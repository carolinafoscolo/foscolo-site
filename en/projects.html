Editorial projects

Building bridges between research, memory and society.

Our projects bring together editorial care, historical research, writing and cultural preservation, transforming ideas, records and experiences into works designed to remain.

Inaugural published project

The first project published by Fóscolo & Company marks the beginning of an independent catalogue dedicated to research, memory, images and the public circulation of ideas.

The book emerges from the relationship between travel, observation, writing and intellectual production, bringing together care, risk and knowledge in an authorial editorial project.

More than a single title, it inaugurates a way of working: independent publications developed with rigor, clarity, careful curation and attention to permanence.

Beyond publishing books

Editorial work also means listening, researching, organizing knowledge, preserving memory and creating meaningful dialogue between authors, readers, institutions and society.

Our editorial process

Research

Understanding the context, sources, questions and objectives that shape each project.

Curation

Selecting, organizing and giving structure to materials with editorial coherence and intellectual responsibility.

Editorial Development

Editing, revision, preparation of texts and construction of a clear, elegant and lasting narrative.

Design

Graphic design and visual identity developed to support reading, clarity and long-term preservation.

Publication

Production of print and digital editions designed for careful circulation.

Permanence

Projects conceived not only for immediate release, but also for future consultation, reading and reference.

Current initiatives

Our first editorial projects focus on history, the history of health, essays, literature, poetry, chronicles, memoirs, institutional memory and the circulation of research beyond specialized spaces.


As the catalogue grows, these initiatives will form the foundation of an independent editorial program committed to autonomy, rigor, clarity, careful curation and long-term cultural value.
