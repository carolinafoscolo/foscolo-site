Todos ou nenhum?

Book I · A matéria aprende a respirar

Maria Carolina Fóscolo

An aircraft goes around. Glass retains stresses after the fire. A sheet bends before becoming a book. Thirteen poems and three literary chronicles explore what changes when the conditions for continuing change.

In editorial preparation. The publisher will announce the release date and purchase details.

How can we change without disappearing?

Matter, bodies and knowledge come together without losing their differences. Iron, water, light, pressure and memory resist easy comparisons.

Images interrupt the journey and alter the pace of the page. The book includes an introduction, prologue, sixteen texts, epilogue and source notes.

Original text in Portuguese

Before the first sentence

The prologue begins with a sheet of paper. Opening a book is already part of the reading experience.

Uma folha sozinha quase não oferece resistência.
Antes da primeira frase, existe esse gesto mínimo.
Separar duas superfícies que estavam encostadas.
Sustentar a abertura.
O resto começa depois.

From the prologue of Todos ou nenhum? — Book I
