Notes on Care, Risk and Knowledge

The first title published by Fóscolo & Company Press marks the beginning of an editorial catalogue dedicated to memory, research, public knowledge and independent publishing.

The book brings together intellectual experience, travel, observation and writing, opening a line of work committed to preserving records, images and reflections that emerge from research in motion.
