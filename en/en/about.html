About Fóscolo & Company Press

Fóscolo & Company Press is an independent publishing house dedicated to works that preserve experiences, expand debates and contribute to the construction of public knowledge.


Founded in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, the press was born from the encounter between research, memory, culture and writing. Its work is guided by the conviction that books are more than objects: they are forms of permanence.


Each publication represents an opportunity to record trajectories, organize knowledge, preserve experiences and broaden dialogue among different communities of readers.


We live in a time marked by an abundance of information and the fragility of memory. Research often remains restricted to specialized circles. Archives preserve stories that are rarely known beyond their own walls. Institutions produce knowledge that does not always reach broader publics. At the same time, individual and collective experiences risk disappearing without record or recognition.


Fóscolo & Company Press was created to work within this space.


We are dedicated to publishing works related to history, memory, cultural heritage, health, the humanities and the multiple ways individuals, communities and institutions give meaning to their experiences.


We value projects that combine intellectual rigor, clarity of communication and social relevance, bringing research closer to public life without reducing the complexity of the subjects addressed.


We understand editorial work as a practice of care: care for authors, sources, writing, readers and the stories entrusted to us. For this reason, each project is developed with attention to editorial quality, ethical commitment and responsibility in the circulation of knowledge.


More than publishing books, we seek to build bridges: between past and present, memory and future, research and society.


We believe some ideas deserve to remain.

Mission

To preserve, organize and expand the circulation of knowledge, memory and experience through editorial projects committed to intellectual rigor, writing quality and dialogue with society.

Vision

To become a reference in the publication of works dedicated to memory, history, the humanities and the public circulation of knowledge, contributing to stronger connections between research, culture and society.


We believe publishing can connect different times, experiences and communities of readers, expanding access to forms of knowledge that help us understand the world and imagine more conscious futures.

Values

Permanence

Publishing preserves experiences, knowledge and reflections beyond their immediate moment.

Rigor

We value intellectual quality, attention to sources, editorial responsibility and care in the construction of each work.

Access

We support the broad circulation of knowledge and stronger connections between intellectual production, cultural institutions and society.

Dialogue

We recognize the value of encounters among different fields of knowledge, life experiences and ways of interpreting the world.

Memory

We understand memory as a collective heritage, essential to understanding the past, reflecting on the present and building the future.

Care

We edit with attention to authors, readers, sources and the stories entrusted to us.

What moves us

We publish because some experiences should not disappear.


We publish because certain research deserves to reach new publics.


We publish because memory, knowledge and culture are fundamental public goods.


We publish because some ideas deserve to remain.
