Editorial Projects

Building bridges between research, memory and society.

Our projects combine editorial work, historical research and cultural preservation, transforming knowledge into publications designed to remain relevant over time.

Inaugural published project

Published title

Notes on Care, Risk and Knowledge

The first project published by Fóscolo & Company Press marks the beginning of a catalogue dedicated to the public circulation of ideas, records, images and research experiences.

The book emerges from the relationship between travel, observation, writing and intellectual production, bringing together memory, care, risk and knowledge in an independent editorial project.

More than a single title, it inaugurates a way of working: careful independent publications designed for permanence.
