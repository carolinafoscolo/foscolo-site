Editorial Projects

Building bridges between research, memory and society.

Our projects combine editorial expertise, historical research and cultural preservation, transforming knowledge into publications that remain relevant over time.

What we do

Publishing

Editorial development of books, e-books, catalogues, essays and scholarly publications.

Oral History

Interviews, life histories and institutional memory projects designed to preserve experiences for future generations.

Institutional Memory

Projects documenting the history, identity and legacy of organizations, research groups and cultural institutions.

Archives & Documentation

Organization, interpretation and dissemination of documentary collections and historical archives.

Cultural Heritage

Editorial initiatives related to museums, architecture, urban history, heritage preservation and cultural landscapes.

Public Scholarship

Transforming academic research into publications accessible to wider audiences while preserving scholarly quality.

Beyond publishing books

Editorial work is also about listening, documenting, organizing knowledge, preserving memory and creating meaningful dialogue between research and society.

Our editorial process

Research

Understanding the historical context, objectives and sources behind every project.

Editorial Development

Editing, revision, narrative structure and editorial preparation.

Design

Graphic design and visual identity developed to support long-term readability and preservation.

Publication

Production of print and digital editions designed for broad circulation.

Dissemination

Strategies that connect publications with readers, researchers and cultural institutions.

Long-term Impact

Projects conceived not only for immediate publication, but also for future consultation and reference.

Current initiatives

Our first editorial projects focus on history, oral history, public health, institutional memory, cultural heritage and public scholarship. As our catalogue grows, these initiatives will form the foundation of a broader editorial program committed to preserving knowledge across generations.
