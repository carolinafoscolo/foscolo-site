Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge

Research, Fragmentos and Images from Brazil

The book

Care begins where certainty ends.

Moving between historical research, essay, poetry, and photography, the book asks how people and institutions learn to continue when protocols no longer contain the world they were designed to explain.

Zika

Pregnancy governed through risk

The tensions between guidance, uncertainty, and public responsibility during a health emergency.

Yellow fever

Protocols and disputed expertise

How clinical and institutional knowledge is reorganized under pressure and disagreement.

Suspected Ebola

Frontline improvisation

The practical intelligence that sustains care when ready-made answers no longer suffice.

Editorial form

Three forms of attention

Research. Archives, oral history, and institutional life reveal how knowledge and care are reorganized under pressure.

Writing. Essays, poems, and fragments approach what evidence alone cannot contain.

Image. Photography observes landscape, structure, and the traces through which thought takes form.

“Crises do not only expose institutional limits. They also reveal the practical intelligence, remembered gestures, and fragile forms of care through which institutions continue to hold.”

About the author

Maria Carolina Fóscolo Gomes

A Brazilian historian and researcher whose work examines public health, institutional knowledge, memory, and care. She is the founder of Fóscolo & Company, an independent publishing project bringing intellectual rigor, visual form, and literary expression into sustained conversation.
