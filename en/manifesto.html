Manifesto

Some stories
should not disappear.

We live surrounded by information.

We produce texts, images, research, records and opinions at unprecedented speed. It has never been easier to publish. It has never been harder to remain.

Every day, new content appears and disappears. Archives are forgotten. Research becomes inaccessible. Experiences accumulated over decades leave few traces. Institutions change. People leave. Stories disperse.

Not everything can be preserved.

But some things must be.

Fóscolo & Company was born from the conviction that publishing remains one of the most powerful forms of permanence humanity has ever created.

Publishing is more than producing books.

Publishing is recognizing value.

It is affirming that a certain experience deserves to be recorded. That a certain work of research deserves to be shared. That a certain memory deserves to continue producing meaning beyond its immediate time.

We are interested in stories that help us understand the world. In research that expands horizons. In essays that sharpen perception. In literary writing that gives form to experience. In testimonies that preserve memory. In archives that hold trajectories. In questions that remain relevant even when answers change.

We believe knowledge should not remain restricted.

We believe memory is a collective heritage.

We believe culture grows stronger when it circulates.

We believe good ideas deserve readers.

Why we publish

To preserve.
To share.
To build bridges.
To expand dialogue.
To transform knowledge into legacy.

Our choice

In a time shaped by speed, we choose permanence.

In a time shaped by excess, we choose care.

In a time shaped by forgetting, we choose memory.

This choice guides every project we develop.

Because some stories should not disappear.
Because some ideas deserve to remain.
