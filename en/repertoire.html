Repertório Fóscolo

References brought back to the table. Readings, photographs and editorial choices acquire meaning through the relationships we build between them.

Saving is not the same as building a repertoire.

A saved image still awaits a question. The work begins when we return to it: what caught our attention, in what context was it made, and what changes beside another image?

Repertório Fóscolo is a series about that return. A detail in a book, a landscape or a framing choice can open a relationship. Naming the source and testing the connection are part of reading.

A reading of the opening of “Missa do Galo”: remembering a conversation is not the same as understanding what it meant.
