Maria Carolina
Fóscolo

Author, editor and undergraduate History student at the Federal University of Minas Gerais. Based in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.

MCFBelo Horizonte / Brasil

Her research brings together the history of health, organisational studies, memory and oral history. From 2023 to 2026, she undertook undergraduate research at FACE/UFMG on Hospital Eduardo de Menezes and institutional responses to health crises.

She founded Fóscolo & Company Edições and wrote Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge. In Todos ou nenhum? — Book I, poetry and literary chronicles bring matter, bodies and knowledge into conversation.

Her work also includes teacher training, cultural mediation and public communication of research.
