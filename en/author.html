Author · Historian · Publisher

Maria Carolina Fóscolo Gomes

Her work examines public institutions, health, memory, and the forms of knowledge and care that emerge under pressure.

A practice between archive, institution, and writing.

A Brazilian historian training at the Federal University of Minas Gerais, Maria Carolina works at the intersection of the history of health, organizational studies, archives, and oral history. Her research follows how institutions learn, improvise, remember, and sustain care when certainty no longer suffices.

She founded Fóscolo & Company to bring intellectual rigor, visual form, and literary sensibility into sustained conversation.

“Research also means attending to what remains at the margins.”
