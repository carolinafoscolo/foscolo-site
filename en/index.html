Publishing is a form of permanence

Books do more than preserve information. They give form to experiences, organize ideas, connect generations and create bridges between research, writing and public life.


At Fóscolo & Company, publishing is understood as an act of care: a way of transforming knowledge, memory and literary creation into lasting works.
