Short biography

Maria Carolina Fóscolo is an author, editor and undergraduate History student at UFMG. She studies health, institutions, memory and knowledge. She founded Fóscolo & Company Edições in Belo Horizonte and is the author of Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge and the forthcoming Todos ou nenhum? — Book I: A matéria aprende a respirar.
