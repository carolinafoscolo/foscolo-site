Short synopsis

Notes on Care, Risk & Knowledge gathers research, fragments, poems, and images produced in Brazil. Moving across public health, institutions, memory, and everyday life, it asks how care is reorganized when protocols and certainty no longer suffice.

About the author

Maria Carolina Fóscolo Gomes is a Brazilian historian and researcher whose work examines public health, institutional knowledge, memory, risk, and care. She is the founder of Fóscolo & Company.
